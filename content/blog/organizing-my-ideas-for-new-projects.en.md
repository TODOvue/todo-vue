Starting a new project always feels exciting… and overwhelming. Ideas come faster than they can be organized. Over the years, I’ve built a method to keep things under control.

## Step 1: Write everything down
Without judging, filtering, or organizing. Just dump the thoughts somewhere safe.

## Step 2: Define the real MVP
Not the dream project.  
Just the smallest version that *proves the idea works*.

## Step 3: Group and prioritize
Related ideas go together. Unnecessary ones get removed.  
Clarity comes from cutting, not adding.

## Step 4: Visual structure
Boards, lists, mind maps… whatever helps me see the big picture.  
Once the structure exists, everything feels lighter.

Good organization makes space for creativity, not the opposite.
