People say users don’t notice details. In my experience, they do. Maybe not consciously, but they *feel* them.

## Spacing that breathes
Good spacing makes an interface feel calm. Tight or inconsistent spacing creates tension, even if the user can’t articulate why.

## Micro-interactions that feel alive
A subtle hover animation or a smooth transition can make a button feel *real*. It adds personality without distracting from functionality.

## Accessible color choices
Color contrast isn’t just a rule. It’s respect for the person using your app.  
Small adjustments can drastically improve usability.

## Why I care
Building UI isn’t only about making things pretty.  
It’s about crafting an experience that feels considered and trustworthy.

And those feelings are built from details barely anyone talks about.
