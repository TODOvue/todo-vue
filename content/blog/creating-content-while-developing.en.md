Writing while developing sounds productive, but it’s way harder than expected.

## The mental switch
Coding and writing use different parts of the mind.  
Switching between them feels like rebooting my brain every time.

## The fear of “not having something important to say”
Content doesn’t have to be deep or brilliant.  
It just has to be honest.

## What keeps me going
I write to record the journey.  
Someone out there might relate or learn from it.  
And future me will definitely appreciate the notes.

Balance is tricky, but the process itself has value.
