Sometimes the most useful lessons come from surprisingly small mistakes. Mine happened earlier this year.

## The unforgettable “oops”
I misread a simple instruction, rushed through something, and created an issue that took longer than it should have to fix. It wasn’t dramatic. Just human.

## What it taught me
- Slow down when it matters
- Read twice
- Rushing rarely saves time

## A gentle reminder
Mistakes don’t define our skill level.  
How we respond to them does.

This one reminded me to work with intention, not anxiety.
