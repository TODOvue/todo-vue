Starting a coding session is never just about opening the editor. For me, it begins long before that. I have a small ritual that helps me shift into a focused, creative mindset.

## The first step: a quiet moment
I take a minute to breathe, stretch a little, and let my mind land. It sounds trivial, but it changes how I approach the next hours.

## The perfect cup
Coffee is a central character in this story. Not because it keeps me awake, but because preparing it forces me to slow down. It sets a tone of intention.

## Making the environment “mine”
I adjust lights, set the temperature of the room, and pick the music. Lo-fi, ambient, or silence. Whatever helps me feel grounded.

## Why it matters
Routines help us tell our brain: *now we create*.  
For me, this ritual transforms coding from a task into something meaningful.

It reminds me that even great software starts with small, mindful moments.
