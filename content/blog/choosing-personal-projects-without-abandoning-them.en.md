Personal projects are exciting, but let’s be honest: many die after a few days. Here’s what helped me choose ones that actually last.

## Pick something that solves a real annoyance
Tiny or huge, it doesn’t matter.  
Annoyances fuel consistency better than inspiration.

## Avoid “ego projects”
Projects created only to look impressive rarely survive.  
Projects created to learn or to help someone usually do.

## Define the meaning behind it
Ask yourself:  
*Why does this matter to me?*  
If there’s no answer, the project won’t live long.

## Accept that small progress is still progress
The goal isn’t speed.  
It’s continuity.

Meaningful ideas deserve time.
